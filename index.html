About Me

I'm Mohamed Amine Djouama, a freelance video editor based in Algeria working on elevating my craft and shaping my distinct visual style.

I focus on creating modern videos that harmonize rhythm, precise cuts, dynamic motion, and visual identity—prioritizing clarity of message and an exceptional viewer experience.

I'm actively growing my portfolio through real and experimental projects in collaboration with content creators, friends, and emerging businesses, striving with every timeline to deliver work that surpasses the last.

📍 Location: Boumerdès / Algeria
